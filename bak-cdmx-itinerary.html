Getting Around

Transportation

🚇
Metro (STC)
The fastest way around the city. Your apartment at Marsella is a 5-min walk to Insurgentes (Line 1, Pink). Clean, frequent, very cheap.
~$0.25 USD per ride · Tap card or tokens · Avoid 7–9 AM / 5–8 PM rush
🚲
Ecobici Bike Share
CDMX has an excellent bike-share network with dedicated lanes on Reforma and throughout Condesa/Roma. Perfect for your neighborhood. 45-min trips are free with a day/week pass.
Register at ecobici.cdmx.gob.mx · Week pass ~$3 USD · Station on Marsella
🚕
Uber / InDrive
Uber works well in CDMX and is much safer than hailing street taxis. Use for trips that are too long to bike, especially at night or with luggage.
$3–8 USD for most crosstown trips · InDrive often cheaper
🏟
Azteca — Match Day
Metro Line 2 (Blue) to Tasqueña, then the Tren Ligero to Estadio Azteca stop. Do not drive or take Uber — streets around the stadium will be gridlocked for hours before and after.
Tren Ligero runs every 5–10 min on match days · Leave 3 hrs before kickoff
🛬
Airport Transfer
On arrival: buy an official taxi voucher inside Terminal 1 (fixed price, much safer). On departure, Uber or a booked taxi from your address. ~30–45 min to/from Juárez.
Official taxi ~$15 USD · Uber slightly cheaper · Allow 90 min on departure day
🚌
Metrobús
BRT buses running on dedicated lanes. Line 1 runs along Insurgentes — useful for Coyoacán and southern neighborhoods. Needs a separate card (buy at any station).
~$0.50 USD · Faster than Metro for north-south trips
Travel Notes

Essentials

🎟
Book Ahead
  • Frida Kahlo Museum — museofridakahlo.org.mx
  • Lucha Libre Tickets — ticketmaster.com.mx
  • Museo Antropología — mna.inah.gob.mx
  • Dinner reservations (Contramar, Rosetta)
💸
Money
  • Withdraw pesos from Banamex / HSBC ATMs
  • Many places are cash-only (markets, taquerias)
  • Notify your bank before traveling
  • $1 USD ≈ 17–18 MXN (May 2026)
☁️
June Weather
  • Highs ~78°F · Lows ~56°F at night
  • Rainy season: afternoon showers daily
  • Pack a compact rain jacket or poncho
  • 7,350 ft altitude — take it easy day one
📱
Apps to Download
  • Uber (reliable, safe)
  • Ecobici (bike share)
  • Google Maps (offline CDMX map)
  • Google Translate (camera mode)
🌮
Don't Miss
  • Tacos al pastor from a street trompo
  • Café de olla (cinnamon-spiced drip coffee)
  • Elote or esquites from a cart
  • Agua de jamaica or horchata
🛡
Safety Tips
  • Condesa, Roma, Polanco, Juárez = very safe
  • Always use Uber over street taxis at night
  • Keep phones in pockets on the Metro
  • Buy water in large jugs — don't drink tap
